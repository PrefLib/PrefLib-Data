Publication Date:

Description: <p>Ranked ballot profiles from Alaska's ranked-choice elections: the special general election of 16 August 2022 for the U.S. House, and the general elections of 8 November 2022 and 5 November 2024. 41 single-winner instant-runoff contests containing 1,986,738 ballots. The general-election contests are those with at least three named candidates: President, U.S. Senate, U.S. House, Governor, and state legislative seats. The source is the cast vote record (Dominion NIST SP 1500-103 JSON) published by the <a href="https://www.elections.alaska.gov/">Alaska Division of Elections</a>. Felix Sargent converted the records to PrefLib; interactive reports are hosted on <a href="https://ranked.vote/">ranked.vote</a>.</p><p>Each ballot is read as Alaska counts it (AS 15.15.350): ambiguous marks are ignored; two consecutive skipped rankings or an overvoted ranking end the ballot; a candidate ranked again is ignored at the later rank. Records the export marks as an invalid contest are left out, as in the official count. All write-ins form one Write-in alternative. Identical rankings are combined with their exact multiplicities, and unranked candidates remain unranked. Ballots with no counted preference are omitted and counted in the crosswalk. 00081-00000042.dat is a CSV crosswalk giving contest IDs, ballot and record counts, source checksums and ranked.vote report links. It is metadata, not a further election.</p>

Required Citations: Alaska Division of Elections. Cast vote records, 2022 special general election and 2022 and 2024 general elections. https://www.elections.alaska.gov/ ; Felix Sargent. ranked.vote: Alaska election reports and PrefLib conversion. https://ranked.vote/
