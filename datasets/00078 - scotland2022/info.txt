Description: <p>Ranked ballot profiles from the Scottish council elections of 5 May 2022: 347 contested wards across all 32 councils, containing 1,857,181 recorded ballots. Eight uncontested wards have no ballot records and are excluded. The source is <a href="https://www.macs.hw.ac.uk/~denis/stv_elections/SC2022/">Denis Mollison's compilation</a> of preference data published by council Returning Officers. Felix Sargent converted the rank-vector files to PrefLib; interactive reports and the source mirror are hosted on <a href="https://stv.vote/">stv.vote</a>.</p><p>Each SOI file preserves observed partial rankings, candidate order and candidate/party labels. Identical rankings are combined with their exact multiplicities. Unranked candidates remain unranked; no bottom ties or additional preferences are inferred. These profiles contain the recorded preferences supplied by the source, not rejected ballot papers or turnout totals. 00078-00000348.dat is a CSV crosswalk giving seats, ballot counts, source paths, checksums and stv.vote report links. It is metadata, not a further election.</p>

Required Citations: Denis Mollison. Scottish Council election 2022. https://www.macs.hw.ac.uk/~denis/stv_elections/SC2022/ ; Felix Sargent. stv.vote: Scottish council election reports and PrefLib conversion. https://stv.vote/
