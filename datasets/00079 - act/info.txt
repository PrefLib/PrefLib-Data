Description: <p>Ranked ballot profiles from the Australian Capital Territory Legislative Assembly elections of 2008, 2012, 2016, 2020 and 2024: 21 electorate contests containing 1,220,223 ballots. The Assembly is elected by Hare-Clark single transferable vote, with five seats per electorate (seven in Molonglo in 2008 and 2012). The source is the ballot paper preference data published by <a href="https://www.elections.act.gov.au/">Elections ACT</a> from its electronic counting system, which records the preferences on every formal ballot paper. Felix Sargent converted the files to PrefLib; interactive reports are hosted on <a href="https://stv.vote/">stv.vote</a>.</p><p>Each paper's preferences are read in order and stop at the first missing number, at a number given to two candidates, or at a candidate numbered twice, as in a Hare-Clark count. Identical rankings are combined with their exact multiplicities, and unranked candidates remain unranked. Candidate labels are the source names with their party group. 00079-00000022.dat is a CSV crosswalk giving seats, ballot and paper counts, source files, checksums and stv.vote report links. It is metadata, not a further election.</p>
